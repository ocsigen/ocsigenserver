```ocaml
val make : 
  ?release:(unit -> unit Lwt.t) ->
  Cohttp.Transfer.encoding ->
  ((string -> unit Lwt.t) -> unit Lwt.t) ->
  t
```
`make ~release encoding writer` makes a response body whose content is generated by `writer write`. `write str` blocks until `str` is fully written. `writer` must free what it holds, such as an open file, even when `write` fails. If the body is not written, for example in the response to a HEAD request, `release ()` is to be called instead, once, to free the same resources (default: nothing). A body whose `writer` writes another body must release that body in `release`.

```ocaml
val of_cohttp : 
  ?release:(unit -> unit Lwt.t) ->
  encoding:Cohttp.Transfer.encoding ->
  Cohttp_lwt.Body.t ->
  t
```
`of_cohttp ~release ~encoding body` is the Cohttp body `body`. By default, it is released by reading it to the end: the whole of a file, or the whole response of a remote server. A producer that can free its body sooner, by closing a connection for example, gives `release`.
